Welcome to ConstructLM

ConstructLM is a privacy-first RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) workspace that runs entirely in your browser. Upload documents, ask questions, and get AI-powered answers with source citations—all while keeping your data completely private.

What is ConstructLM?

ConstructLM transforms how you interact with your documents using AI. Unlike traditional RAG systems that send your data to external servers for processing, ConstructLM uses local browser-based embeddings (Transformers.js) to keep your documents completely private. Only the final queries are sent to AI providers (Gemini or Cerebras) for generating responses.

Whether you're researching technical documentation, analyzing business reports, or studying academic papers, ConstructLM provides intelligent answers backed by exact source citations from your uploaded documents.

Key Benefits

  • Privacy-First Architecture - Documents are embedded locally in your browser using Transformers.js. No document data leaves your device
  • Dual AI Models - Switch between Google Gemini and Cerebras AI for chat responses with multiple model options
  • Smart RAG Search - Diversified retrieval ensures coverage from all uploaded documents with relevance scoring
  • Source Citations - Every answer includes clickable citations showing the exact source text used
  • Multi-Format Support - Upload TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, and PDF files
  • Chat History - Automatic session management with persistent chat history
  • Markdown Rendering - Rich formatting in responses including tables, code blocks, and lists
  • Offline Embeddings - Once the model is downloaded (~25MB), embeddings work offline
  • IndexedDB Storage - All documents and embeddings stored locally in your browser
  • Responsive Design - Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices

How It Works

ConstructLM operates through a simple workflow:

  1. Upload Documents

    Add your documents (TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, PDF). They are chunked and embedded locally using Transformers.js.

  2. Ask Questions

    Type your question. The system searches your document embeddings locally to find relevant context.

  3. Get AI Answers

    The relevant context is sent to your chosen AI model (Gemini or Cerebras) which generates an answer with citations.

  4. Explore Sources

    Click on citation badges to see the exact source text that was used to generate the answer.

Privacy Guarantee

Your Data Stays Private

ConstructLM uses local embeddings (Transformers.js) to process your documents entirely in your browser. Only the final query and retrieved context snippets are sent to AI providers. Your full documents never leave your device.

System Requirements

  • Browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari (latest versions)
  • RAM: Minimum 4GB recommended
  • Storage: ~25MB for embedding model (cached after first download)
  • Internet: Required for AI model API calls and initial model download
  • API Keys: Gemini API key and/or Cerebras API key
